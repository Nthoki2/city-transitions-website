9 November 2026 · Rundu, Namibia

Community consultation in Rundu under ISUD II

As part of the Inclusive Sustainable Development Project (ISUD II), community members in Rundu came together for an open-air consultation led by the project lead Dr. Alexander Jachnow. Residents shared their experiences and priorities, which will help guide how the project supports inclusive and sustainable urban development. CiTra is working on ISUD II together with INTEGRATION GmbH and GIZ.

26–29 October 2026 · Chicago, USA

CiTra at the Chicago Expo 2026

Between 26 and 29 October, Dr.-Ing Dorcas Nthoki Nyamai from CiTra took part in the Chicago Expo 2026. The participation was organised by the German American Chamber of Commerce under the theme of Sustainability.
