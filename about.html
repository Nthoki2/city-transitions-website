Who we are

CiTra (City Transitions) is a consultancy that supports governments, funders, development cooperation agencies and communities in making cities more sustainable, inclusive and resilient. We combine research, training and hands-on implementation, from mobility, decarbonisation and informal settlement upgrading to sustainable building materials, drawing on experience in 22 countries across four continents.
