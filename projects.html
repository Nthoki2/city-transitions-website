Current projects

Current project · Bangladesh, Nepal and Sri Lanka

CoHere

A regional initiative of the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC) on climate mobility and stronger municipal systems. CiTra is drawing out implementation lessons from the Shompritee pilot in Jashore, Bangladesh, and other municipal approaches, to inform a gender-responsive and socially inclusive approach in Nepal, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh.

Current project · Rundu, Namibia

ISUD II

Inclusive Sustainable Development Project, together with INTEGRATION GmbH and GIZ. The project improves living and housing conditions in Namibia’s informal settlements.
